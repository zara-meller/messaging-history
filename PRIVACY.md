# Privacy

Messaging History has no server and collects nothing.

- When you click the icon, it reads the current tab's address and, on a homepage, its headline, subheadline and buttons. This stays in your browser.
- It sends the domain you're looking at to the Internet Archive's Wayback Machine (web.archive.org) to get archived copies of that homepage. That is the only request it makes.
- Settings and results are saved in your browser's local extension storage. Results expire after 7 days.
- No analytics, no tracking, no accounts. Nothing is sold or shared.

Questions: open an issue on this repo.
